HotelCraft Support

Help for the HotelCraft iPhone app and the BMI Maintenance website.

Getting an account

Accounts are created by your hotel's maintenance manager, supervisor, or administrator. Sign in to the app with the same username and password you use on bmimaintenance.com.

Forgot your password?

Use Forgot password if your account has an email address, or ask your supervisor to reset it.

Common questions

  • I can't see a request. Each account only sees its own hotel location. Pull down on the list to refresh.
  • Where did a job go? Jobs awaiting parts move to the On Hold tab; completed jobs are under Done for two weeks and always in room history.
  • The scanner won't start. Allow camera access in Settings › HotelCraft › Camera.
  • A part won't add to a job. Check its count in Inventory; receive stock first if it shows zero.
